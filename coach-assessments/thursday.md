Political
- censorship/denial of access in countries with censor programmes
    -> impacts ability to provide access to archived material to anyone 
    -> recently banned in Indonesia and Iraq (2023, 2024)
- stores sensitive U.S. government material (e.g. FBI documents)
    -> those organisations will have an issue -> friction between the Internet Archive and the current administration
- publicly pro-piracy: further friction with U.S. government's anti-piracy measures
    - advocates that it negatively impacts the Internet as something to preserve

Economic
- little economic pressure on the Internet Archive, it sits outside economic fluctuations, being a non-profit organisation

Social
- public has a positive image, moral mission in preserving forgotten and potentially lost media
- gained more support after facing copyright challenges from media giants
- people may be adverse to their personal data being stored on the archive
    -> archive is transparent about what they store however

Technological
- the amount and variety of content created daily on the Internet has increased significantly, and continues to increase
    -> Internet Archive needs to constantly innovate in data storage capabilities
        -> MB -> TB -> PB, invest money and effort into securing these

Strategic next steps:
- handling increased quantity of content to archive as Internet continues expanding
- re-evaluate how they present the archive across different global regions
    - filter content depending on geographical location of a user
