The Internet Archive

Origins
- founded in 1996
- established as a non-profit digital library 
- goal of preserving records of websites and digital content

History
- began archiving websites in the late 1990s
- in late 1999, expanded collections to include audio, video and software
- launched the Wayback Machine in 2001
- faced copyright and digital lending legal challenges from publishing houses and music publishers in the 2020s

Curent operations
- maintains a large digital library of books, media and software
- collaborates with libraries, archives and research institutions on preservation projects
- funded through donations and grants
