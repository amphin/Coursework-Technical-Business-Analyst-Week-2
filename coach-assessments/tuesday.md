SWOT analysis of Netflix

Strengths:
- global brand recognition
- broad customer base
- extensive library of digital content

Weaknesses:
- dependence on subscription model
- high content production costs

Opportunities:
- growth in emerging countries developing Internet connectivity
- cheaper subscription plans with advertisements
- leveraging existing IPs to create new content

Threats:
- intense competition from numerous emerging streaming platforms
- pressure to obtain and maintain exclusive streaming rights
- economic uncertainty restricts consumer choice to keep subscriptions

Netflix's extensive library of content exclusive to its platform provides a significant competitive advantage in the streaming market, having a near-monopoly in the early days of the market thanks to its first-mover advantage. This allowed the company to build strong global brand recognition and a broad customer base. However the streaming market has since become incredibly competitive in the past few years, with television providers entering the market with their own streaming services. This has locked valuable IPs as they took the exclusive streaming rights. Coupled with economic uncertainty, the company faces growing pressure to secure and maintain attractive content while managing high production costs. However, expanding into emerging markets with rising Internet connectivity provides access to millions of potential new customers. Its new advertisement-supported subscription plan also allows attracting more price-sensitive customers.
